# Chapter 1 enemy artwork and replacements

The supplied transparent 64×64 APNGs are copied unchanged to the monster assets: Headwind (two frames), Bat (four frames), Snake (four frames). All loop indefinitely at the supplied 125 ms per frame. The existing image renderer preserves their animation and uses pixelated scaling in main and compact battles.

Chapter 1 native roster: Rat, Bat, Snake, Goblin, Wolf, Giant Fire Beetle, Giant Spider, Worg. Headwind remains the recurring enemy across all chapters and now uses the supplied animation.

The existing Bat species replaces Stirge. Snake replaces Kobold's roster record and retains its authored base HP/attack, with beast type. Stirge and Kobold are removed from the active species list. The save compatibility boundary maps their old IDs in encounter bags and saved fights; battle levels, damage fraction, XP, chapter miles and player HP are preserved. Rewards are still determined by each enemy's own level.

The autoplay preview includes Bat, Snake, and a mixed Headwind/Bat/Snake battle for art review. Production encounters continue to use the normal chapter pools and random group rules.
