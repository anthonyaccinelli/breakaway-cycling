# Boss behaviors and Boss Mode

Chapter and Summit Bosses repeat 20 seconds of normal attack, a five-second brace warning, an earned ten-second stagger, and five seconds of Armored Stance recovery. Regular mobs retain their existing combat.

Brace at or below 50% FTP for at least three of the five warning seconds. The special hit deals eight seconds of the boss's normal DPS, reduced by 75% when braced. A successful brace opens ten seconds of double damage with no incoming attacks; a missed brace proceeds directly to recovery. During armor, neither side deals damage. At or below 50% FTP (including fully stopped), recover 5 HP per second, capped at maximum HP. Phase timers use active battle time and persist with the battle save. No trainer load changes are issued by phases.

Summit Bosses flee after 15 seconds continuously stopped (cadence zero and power below 10 W). Pedaling resets the counter. Armored Stance suspends it. Fleeing grants no victory reward and preserves remaining player HP.

Choose Boss Mode on the Route screen or Ride Mode panel. Pedal for a ten-minute warmup, then challenge the current chapter boss. Warmup awards normal pedaling XP, suppresses random encounters, persists across sessions, and can be skipped. Victory awards normal boss rewards but never unlocks a chapter or changes chapter mileage. Fight again starts another warmup. Adventure continues to require chapter mileage as before.

Automated coverage includes phase boundaries, brace success/failure, healing and caps, double damage, fleeing and recovery exemption, tick-size consistency, defeat, and practice progression isolation. Hardware resistance behavior requires trainer ride testing.
