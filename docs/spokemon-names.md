# Spokemon Names

**Catalogue:** Spokemon Names  
**Game:** Pedalkin (working title)  
**Creatures:** Spokemon (working title)  
**Status:** Working-name catalogue; none of the names or concepts below are final  
**Started:** October 31, 2026

A growing reference for the creature-collector game's species names, organized by biome. Preserve names as candidates until individually approved; no design or implementation work is implied by adding a name here.

**Highlighted candidates from the first draft:** Pipcone, Mossprout, Fernbit, Hootwig, Glimmoth, and Elderoot (favorites expressed in the brainstorming draft, not formally approved over the others).

### Direction / notes

- Spokemon are intended to be friendly, approachable woodland creatures, with personalities and discovery-driven encounters rather than combat.
- A species may appear with different personalities. Do not permanently assign one personality by its name alone.
- Keep creature rarity separate from encounter probability and biome terrain difficulty.
- Artwork, animations, evolution rules, individual capture behaviors, and any copyright/trademark checks remain to be defined.
- Add further biomes as dedicated sections below this one.
