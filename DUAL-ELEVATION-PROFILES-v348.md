# Breakaway Cycling v3.4.8 — Dual elevation overlay

- Top graph follows the rider over a 5-mile window; after 1.2 seconds at >=6% gradient it zooms to 1 mile.
- Returns to 5 miles after 2.5 seconds below 5% grade (hysteresis).
- Bottom graph shows entire fixed route or imported GPX, with rider progress marker.
- Endless routes hide the full-route graph; top graph remains visible.
- Graphs use independent rider-centered coordinates and do not follow the main inspector cursor.
- All previous metric-selection controls remain available.

Start using `Start GPX Trainer with Overlay.command` as before.
