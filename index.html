Drivetrain & shifting

Use your bike’s physical shifters. In SIM mode, Breakaway sends the route gradient directly to your smart trainer; virtual gear resistance adjustments are disabled.

Virtual shifting

Trainer holds the requested wattage by adjusting resistance as cadence changes. Route elevation does not affect resistance. You can change watts during the ride. Shifting is disabled for resistance in this mode.

In SIM mode, virtual gears change gradient-equivalent trainer resistance with ramping and a 300 W soft test governor. In experimental ERG mode, gear and slope calculate a target power command; this is not Zwift’s real shifting algorithm and can feel unnatural. Click requires verified BLE packets. Start with low resistance and test cautiously.

Rider and trainer settings

Live mode only: a connected Direto XR-T with real FTMS power telemetry and granted control is required to start. Route speed is estimated from measured watts and simplified physics, not GPS. Zwift Click decoding remains experimental. Test carefully and keep a physical way to stop the trainer.

Local ride history

Completed rides are saved on this device in your browser. Download TCX files anytime to upload manually to your preferred fitness platform, or delete individual rides. Clearing browser site data or using private browsing may erase this history. Export important rides for backup.
